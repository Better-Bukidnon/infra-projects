About the data

The list is copied once a day from the BetterGov.ph DPWH API, the same data behind Bisto.ph. Budgets, contractors and statuses are shown as published. The city or municipality and the category are worked out from each project's description, and pins that fall outside Bukidnon are left off the map.

Project data is published by DPWH and collected by Bisto.ph. Locations, budgets and progress may be out of date or wrong. To report a problem with a project, use Bisto.ph.
